SIGNALS IN THE DATA

Three angles.
One customer story.

01 / PORTFOLIO

Product count matters.

One- and two-product customers show the highest observed churn rates in this dataset.

27.84% at 2 products
02 / BEHAVIOR

Activity has peaks.

Churn rises in the 10–19 and 40–49 transaction bands; the first band has 100 customers.

49.55% at 40–49 transactions
03 / SEGMENTS

Balance separates risk.

Zero-balance customers churn more often than customers with positive balances.

36.15% zero balance

Descriptive patterns in this dataset; they show association, not cause.
